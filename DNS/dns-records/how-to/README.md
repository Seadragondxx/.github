## What is a Zone Apex?

The zone apex (also called root domain or naked domain) is the highest level of your domain. For example:
- `example.com` is the zone apex
- `www.example.com` is a subdomain
- `blog.example.com` is a subdomain

Zone apex records are critical for making your domain accessible to visitors.
